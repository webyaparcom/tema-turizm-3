Questions

Before you arrive

Tap a strip; the answer opens sideways.

01spaDo you run the balloon flights?

Balloon flights are run by licensed balloon operators, not by the house; flights depend on the weather and may be cancelled. On request we share the contact details of licensed operators; tickets and payment are arranged directly with them.

02spaCan I watch the balloons from the house?

On flight mornings the sunrise terrace opens at 05:30 with tea and blankets ready. Some mornings there are no flights.

03spaAre the rooms cool or warm?

Rock rooms stay naturally cool in summer; in winter there is underfloor heating and wool blankets.

04spaAre the prices final?

Prices on the site are examples. Your booking request is final once the house confirms it in writing by e-mail or WhatsApp.

05spaIs the valley walk hard?

Routes are 4–9 km with some dirt slopes. The route note lists distance, gradient and water points.
